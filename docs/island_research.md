# Island research notes (Fraser / K'gari and Moreton / Mulgumpin)

Collected Oct 2026 from Parks Queensland search snippets, allaboutfraser.com, 4x4australia,
the Tangalooma 4WD guide, Wikipedia and 4WD forum reports. Distances are approximate km along
the beach from the southern tip (+/- 3-5 km). Used to place features on the scaled-down maps.

## Fraser Island

- About 123 km long, up to 22 km wide. Ocean (surf) east coast = 75 Mile Beach, a gazetted road
  (80 km/h, 40 km/h zones at townships / Eli Creek / Maheno). Calm Great Sandy Strait on the west.
- Drive roughly 2-3 h either side of low tide. Hard dark wet sand between waterline and high tide
  mark; dry upper sand is soft. Typical tyre pressures 16-20 psi, 12 psi to unbog / for the worst bits.

## Moreton Island

- About 37 km long, widest in the north (fans out to Cape Moreton / North Point). Ocean beach on
  the east, Moreton Bay on the west. Mount Tempest ~285 m, a forested coastal dune.
- Beach 60 km/h, inland 30 km/h, townships 20 km/h. 18-20 psi. Fuel only at Bulwer.
- Avoid beaches ~2 h either side of high tide.
